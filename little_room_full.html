Single-file local app. Room, shop, inventory, XP, Sparks, streaks, freeze, sleep, captions, camera capture, Discord upload, voice, reminders and backup are included.

Shared accounts/leaderboard require a backend and are intentionally not simulated. Keep your webhook private.
